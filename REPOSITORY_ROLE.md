- Tier: 6 — IP/commercialization support application
- Lifecycle: Active commercial-support implementation

- Historical/reference: No

## Authority
- Identity: CONSUMER
- State: CONSUMER

- Provenance: CONSUMER/PRODUCER for commercialization records
- Execution: OWNER only within its commercialization workflow

## Role
This repository packages technical-asset intake, AST analysis, screening valuation, licensing workflow, buyer matching, and human compliance gates.

It is a business-support application, not the constitutional runtime.

## Boundary
Screening valuation is not a formal appraisal. Buyer matching is not proof of buyer intent. Legal documents are planning outputs, not legal advice or authority. Export-control/compliance claims require actual evidence and human approval.

No commercial workflow may redefine ArgCore contracts.

## Promotion
Any runtime-governance mechanism discovered here must be promoted into Arg/ArgCore through the normal provenance and verification process.

FINAL RULE: commercial packaging consumes governance; it does not own the substrate.
